# Guild event and war pilot

Priority: P2  
Owner: unassigned  
Depends on: 02-production-host-rehearsal, 03-discord-staging-acceptance,
07-discord-delivery-recovery-staging

## Problem

Synthetic tests do not establish that officers and members can operate the
complete workflow in a real guild.

## Scope

- Run a representative event and war with officers and members.
- Review privacy, retention, recovery, and support expectations.
- Convert discovered issues into linked, scoped tasks.

## Acceptance criteria

- The event and war complete without known data or permission leakage.
- Guild operators explicitly accept the documented operating model or record
  concrete follow-up tasks.

## Validation

Record participant roles, test cases, outcomes, and task links without storing
personal or guild-private content in the repository.
