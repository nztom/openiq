# Post-pilot capacity and operations observation

Priority: P3  
Owner: unassigned  
Depends on: 09-guild-event-and-war-pilot

## Problem

The application targets small guilds, but its real workload, queue behavior,
retention settings, and operational burden have not been observed after use.

## Scope

- Observe dashboard response time, queue age/failures, storage growth, retention
  outcomes, and routine operator work through a pilot period.
- Identify measured bottlenecks and convert only supported findings into focused
  engineering tasks.

## Acceptance criteria

- A short, sanitised observation record captures workload and operating results.
- Any capacity or observability improvement has its own scoped todo document.

## Validation

Use normal pilot telemetry and operator notes; do not commit member data,
credentials, or raw guild exports.
