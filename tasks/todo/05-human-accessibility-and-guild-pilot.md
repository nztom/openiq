# Human accessibility review and guild pilot

Priority: P2  
Owner: unassigned  
Depends on: 02-production-host-rehearsal, 03-discord-staging-acceptance

## Problem

Automated accessibility checks and synthetic workflows cannot establish real
screen-reader usability or guild acceptance.

## Scope

- Have a screen-reader user assess announcements, focus order, errors, dialogs,
  and dynamic dashboard workflows.
- Run a complete event and war pilot with officers and members.
- Review privacy, retention, recovery, and support expectations; turn feedback
  into separately owned tasks.

## Acceptance criteria

- Findings, severities, and remediation decisions are recorded.
- At least one representative event/war completes without data or permission
  leakage.
- Guild operators explicitly accept the documented operating model.

## Validation

Record participant roles, test cases, outcomes, and follow-up task links without
placing personal or guild-private content in the repository.
